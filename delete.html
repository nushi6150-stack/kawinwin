Account Deletion Request

At KawinWin, we respect your privacy and your right to control your personal information. You can request the deletion of your account and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your KawinWin account, follow these steps:

  • Send an email to our support team.
  • Use the subject: KawinWin Account Deletion Request.
  • Provide your registered email address or username.
  • Our team will verify your request.
Request Account Deletion

What Data Will Be Deleted?

After your request is verified, we will delete personal information associated with your account that is not required to be retained by law.

  • Account profile information.
  • Personal details associated with your account.
  • Other eligible personal information.

Financial Records and Legal Requirements

Certain financial transaction records may need to be retained for legal, regulatory, security, or fraud-prevention purposes.

Important: Account deletion does not automatically cancel outstanding financial obligations or erase records that must legally be retained.

Deletion Processing Time

We will review and process verified deletion requests within the applicable legal timeframe. If certain information must be retained, we will restrict its use as required by law.
